USAGE MONITOR

Insights

Plan history, alerts and recording health.

Plan history

Observed quota percentages, separated by account and reset window. Up to 31 days retained. Account references are pseudonymous; unidentified accounts cannot be separated reliably. These readings are not token counts or costs.

Notification centre

Network budget

Captured per-app traffic across all apps, including apps excluded from the network list. Only traffic observed while recording is included. This is a partial device total, not your ISP bill.

Diagnostics

The support report contains version, platform, provider status and timestamps. No credentials, account identities, paths, domains or notification text.

Display preferences
